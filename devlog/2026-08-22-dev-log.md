# 2026-08-22

Split the hexapod stack out of the mjlab fork, starting with the VVAE.

## Moved

`hexai` exists. The geometric VVAE, its loader, the geometry and the schedulers came across, then
the hexapod asset. Everything hexai imports from mjlab turned out to be upstream, so it depends on
the published package rather than the fork — no editable install, and the repo is cloneable.

Layering settled as `utilities` → `data` → `models`, one direction only. Geometry, camera
intrinsics and the schedulers all moved out of the VVAE on the same test: would a second model
want this?

32 of 35 hexapod geometry tests pass here; three cross-check constants against `train_hexapod*.py`
and skip until those scripts move.

## VVAE, before the move

Cut the loss registry down to mse, the schedulers to three, and deleted a 58-line commented-out
block. `AdaptiveRestarts` gained warmup, two exponential averages, a probe window and a decay sized
to the run — the last two mattered: a one-epoch verdict on a restart had been walking the ceiling
from 3e-3 to 1.4e-6, and a fixed decay bottomed out at epoch 55 of 80.

Added the metric that can see the hypothesis. `lift_agreement` is measured only over observed
voxels, so it was structurally blind to the unknown-space work; `invented_density` is not.

## Measured

The decoder puts **7.3x more density where the render cannot see it** — 0.375 in samples past the
first surface against 0.051 in visible ones, across 69% of every ray. That is the widening bar in
the top-down plots. Rays themselves terminate sharply, 0.03 m spread against a 0.25 m depth error,
so it is not hedging under uncertainty: density behind the first surface simply has no gradient.

## Running

The `jitter x unknown` sweep, 4 cells at 80 epochs, still against the mjlab copy.
